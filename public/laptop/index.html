Make it part of your laptop

The Windows companion runs in your tray with optional launch at sign-in. Everyday mode adds hinge-driven shading over your real apps. Live fold mode bends a local capture of your desktop with native Windows graphics.

Live fold is a visual mode: click once to return to the unwarped desktop before interacting. Supported hinge sensors drive it automatically; manual controls remain available on other laptops. The browser preview uses the slider, not a physical hinge sensor.
